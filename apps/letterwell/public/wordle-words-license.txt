Wordle helper word lists (wordle-en.txt, wordle-bg.txt)

Each file lists the five-letter words from Letterwell's dictionaries (english-words.txt,
bulgarian-words.txt; MIT licensed, see their license files). Words above the "---" line are
the ones that also appear in a word-frequency list built from film and TV subtitles, in order
of frequency; the words below it are the rest, in alphabetical order. A handful of slurs are
left out.

The frequency order comes from FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave
(https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords), whose word lists are licensed under
Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
These two files are adapted from that work and are shared under the same license.

Word meanings (public/meanings/) come from Wiktionary (https://en.wiktionary.org/),
via the wiktextract-based dictionaries at https://github.com/Vuizur/Wiktionary-Dictionaries.
They are shortened, and are available under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0
licence (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/) and the GNU FDL, like Wiktionary.
Rebuild them with: node scripts/build-meanings.mjs <path to that repository>
