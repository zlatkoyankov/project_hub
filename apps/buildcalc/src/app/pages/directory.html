Choosing a material calculator

Choose a calculator based on the material and measurements you have. Gravel and mulch estimates use area and depth to estimate volume; the gravel calculator can also estimate weight and cost when you enter product details. The paint calculator estimates coverage from wall dimensions and coats. Tile and concrete calculators use the dimensions and material settings shown on their forms, while the square-footage calculator combines rectangular rooms.

These results are planning estimates, not purchase guarantees. Check units, product coverage, package sizes, and site conditions before ordering. Browse the material guides for measuring tips and advice on allowances for cuts, uneven surfaces, and product variation.
