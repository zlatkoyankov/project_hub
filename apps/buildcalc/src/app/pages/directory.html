Which calculator for which job

Every calculator shows its formula, the figures it uses and where they come from, and works in feet and inches or in meters. Pick the one for the material you are buying:

Floors

Tiles and laminate or vinyl plank are counted from the room size and the box, with about 10% extra for cuts. Tile adhesive and grout come in kg per m² from the trowel notch and the joint, and self-leveling compound or screed from the thickness: about 1.5 to 1.6 kg per m² for every millimeter.

Walls

Paint works from the wall area, the coats and the coverage on the can. Wallpaper is counted in full-height strips, not area. Drywall adds the studs, track, screws and tape, insulation boards the supplies for an exterior system, and the brick and block calculator the mortar and packs: about 6.9 modular bricks per sq ft, or 60 UK bricks per m².

Concrete and yard

Concrete, gravel and mulch are all area × depth, in cubic yards or cubic meters; the gravel calculator also gives the weight in tons. The square footage calculator adds up several rooms or areas first.

The results are planning estimates, not purchase guarantees. Check the product's data sheet and the pack size before ordering. The material guides explain how to measure and how much extra to allow.
