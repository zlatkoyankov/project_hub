GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

A few helpful notes before you get started.

Are BuildCalc estimates exact?

No. They are arithmetic estimates based on the measurements and assumptions you provide. Product coverage, material density, site conditions, and cutting waste vary.

Should I add extra material?

Many projects need some allowance for cuts, spillage, or uneven areas. Choose an allowance that suits the project and verify it with product instructions or a qualified supplier.

Are my measurements sent to a server?

No. Calculations run in your browser. BuildCalc does not send calculator inputs to a server.
