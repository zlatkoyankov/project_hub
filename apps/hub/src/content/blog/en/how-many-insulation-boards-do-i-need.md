Whether you are adding rigid foam to a basement wall or wrapping a house in an exterior insulation system, the board count comes from the same simple sum. The supplies around the boards are where estimates usually go wrong.

## Step 1: the area to insulate

Measure each wall: length × height. Add the walls together and subtract the doors and windows. For a house, the perimeter times the wall height gives the gross area.

A house with 1,000 sq ft of wall and 150 sq ft of doors and windows has **850 sq ft** to insulate.

## Step 2: add waste and divide by the board size

Boards are cut at corners, around openings and at the top and bottom of the wall. About 5% is enough for plain walls; use more for walls with many corners and openings.

| Board | Face area |
| --- | --- |
| 4 × 8 ft sheet | 32 sq ft |
| 2 × 8 ft board | 16 sq ft |
| 1 × 0.5 m EPS facade board | 0.5 m² (5.4 sq ft) |
| 1.25 × 0.6 m XPS board | 0.75 m² |
| 1.2 × 0.6 m mineral wool slab | 0.72 m² |

With 4 × 8 ft sheets: 850 × 1.05 = 892.5 sq ft ÷ 32 = 27.9, so **28 sheets**. At 2 in thick that is about 149 cu ft of insulation.

## Step 3: the supplies for exterior insulation

An exterior insulation system (EIFS, called ETICS in Europe) glues the boards to the wall, anchors them, and covers them with a base coat with reinforcing mesh. Typical figures for a cement-based system:

- **Adhesive:** about 5.5 kg per m² of board (about 1.1 lb per sq ft).
- **Base coat:** about 4.0 kg per m² (0.8 lb per sq ft), with the mesh pressed into it.
- **Mesh:** the wall area plus about 10% for overlaps.
- **Anchors:** about 6 per m² on low buildings, more at corners and on tall or windy sites.

For the 850 sq ft wall (about 79 m²) that is roughly 960 lb of adhesive, 700 lb of base coat, 935 sq ft of mesh and 475 anchors. Many products work for both gluing and the base coat, which makes ordering easier.

## What is not included

- **Window reveals** need narrow strips of thinner insulation and corner beads with mesh.
- **Starter tracks, corner beads and drip edges** are counted in running feet, not by area.
- **The finish coat** (render or acrylic finish) has its own consumption per m².

The thickness itself depends on your climate, the wall and local energy rules. This guide counts materials for a thickness you have already chosen.
