Most of my working life has gone into large web applications: systems with many screens, many users and many years of history. That work is satisfying, but it is rarely something you can show to a friend and say "try this".

Zyankov.eu is the opposite. Each project here does one clear job:

- **Letterwell** answers the question every word game raises: which words can you make from these letters, and how many points are they worth? It works in English and Bulgarian.
- **BuildCalc** answers the questions every home project raises: how much concrete, how many boxes of tile, how many tins of paint?

## The rules I follow

1. **One tool, one job.** No dashboards, no feature lists. Open the page and use it.
2. **It runs in your browser.** The letters you type and the measurements you enter are not sent to a server.
3. **No account.** Nothing to sign up for, nothing to remember.
4. **Show the assumptions.** Every calculator explains its formula and its limits, because an estimate is only useful if you know what it assumes.
5. **Keep it maintained.** Small tools still need care: tests, fixes and updates when something changes.

## What comes next

New articles will explain the maths behind the calculators and the techniques behind word games, with examples you can check in the tools. New tools will follow the same rules. If there is something you would find useful, write to me at [hello@zyankov.eu](mailto:hello@zyankov.eu).
