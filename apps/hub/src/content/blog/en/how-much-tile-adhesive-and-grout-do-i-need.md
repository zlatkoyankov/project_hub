Counting tiles is the easy part. The bags of adhesive and grout are where most people guess, and they either run out halfway through or carry three unopened bags back to the store. Both can be worked out in a few minutes once you know what drives them.

## Adhesive depends on the trowel, not the tile count

Tile adhesive (thinset mortar) is spread with a notched trowel, and the size of the notch decides how thick the bed is. Bigger tiles need a deeper notch so that the adhesive reaches the whole back of the tile.

Typical consumption for a cement adhesive, from Ceresit CM 11's data sheet:

| Tile size (longest side) | Trowel notch | Adhesive per m² | Per sq ft |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| up to 4 in (10 cm) | 4 mm (5/32 in) | 2.0 kg | 0.41 lb |
| up to 6 in (15 cm) | 6 mm (1/4 in) | 2.7 kg | 0.55 lb |
| up to 10 in (25 cm) | 8 mm (5/16 in) | 3.4 kg | 0.70 lb |
| about 12 in (30 cm) | 10 mm (3/8 in) | 4.2 kg | 0.86 lb |
| larger | 12 mm (1/2 in) | 4.8 kg | 0.98 lb |

Other brands are similar, but check the data sheet of the bag you buy. An uneven floor or wall takes more.

## Grout depends on how many joints there are

Grout fills the joints, so what matters is how much joint there is per square foot. The formula manufacturers such as Mapei, Weber and Sika publish is:

**grout (kg/m²) = (tile length + tile width) ÷ (tile length × tile width) × joint width × joint depth × 1.6**

All sizes go in millimeters, the joint depth is normally the tile thickness, and 1.6 is the typical density of cement grout in kg per liter.

The first part of the formula is why small tiles use so much more grout:

- **12 × 12 in tile**, 3/8 in thick, 1/8 in joints: about **0.32 kg per m²** (0.065 lb per sq ft).
- **2 × 2 in mosaic**, 1/4 in thick, 1/8 in joints: about **1.27 kg per m²**, four times as much.

## Worked example: a 120 sq ft floor

A kitchen floor of 120 sq ft with 12 × 12 in porcelain tiles, 3/8 in thick, 1/8 in joints and 10% waste:

1. Area with waste: 120 × 1.10 = 132 sq ft, or about 12.3 m².
2. Adhesive with a 3/8 in (10 mm) notch: 12.3 × 4.2 = 51.5 kg, about 114 lb. In 50 lb bags that is **3 bags**.
3. Grout: 12.3 × 0.32 = 3.9 kg, about 8.6 lb. **One 25 lb bag** is plenty.

## Tips that save a trip to the store

- **Buy grout from one batch.** Colours can differ slightly between batches.
- **Keep a little of each.** A spare bag of adhesive and a small amount of grout make repairs easy later.
- **Back-butter large tiles.** For tiles over about 24 in, spread a thin layer on the back as well. That uses more adhesive than the table shows.
- **Mix only what you can use.** Most adhesives stay workable for an hour or two once mixed.
