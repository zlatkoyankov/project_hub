2027 is a good year for long weekends. In the **US**, 10 of the 12 federal days off fall on a Monday or a Friday. In **England and Wales**, Easter and Christmas each give a four-day break without taking any leave.

Full lists, with calendar files: [US federal holidays 2027](/us-federal-holidays-2027/) and [UK bank holidays 2027](/uk-bank-holidays-2027/).

## United States

Three-day weekends with no leave:

| Weekend | Holiday |
| --- | --- |
| Fri 1 – Sun 3 January | New Year's Day |
| Sat 16 – Mon 18 January | Martin Luther King, Jr. Day |
| Sat 13 – Mon 15 February | Washington's Birthday |
| Sat 29 – Mon 31 May | Memorial Day |
| Fri 18 – Sun 20 June | Juneteenth (moved from Saturday 19th) |
| Sat 3 – Mon 5 July | Independence Day (moved from Sunday 4th) |
| Sat 4 – Mon 6 September | Labor Day |
| Sat 9 – Mon 11 October | Columbus Day |
| Fri 24 – Sun 26 December | Christmas (moved from Saturday 25th) |
| Fri 31 December – Sun 2 January | New Year's Day 2028 (moved from Saturday) |

Two Thursdays become four-day weekends with **one day of leave** on the Friday:

- **Veterans Day**, Thursday 11 November: take Friday 12 November.
- **Thanksgiving**, Thursday 25 November: take Friday 26 November.

### Why 24 and 31 December

A federal holiday on a Saturday is observed on the Friday before, and one on a Sunday on the Monday after. Christmas 2027 is a Saturday, so the day off is Friday 24 December. New Year's Day 2028 is also a Saturday, so it is observed on **Friday 31 December 2027**. That gives federal offices 12 days off in calendar 2027 instead of 11.

These are the rules for federal employees. Private employers, schools and banks choose their own days.

## United Kingdom

The bank holidays differ between England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland. All of them get these long weekends:

- **Good Friday, 26 March**: Easter is on 28 March. England, Wales and Northern Ireland also have Easter Monday, 29 March, so that is a four-day weekend. Scotland has Good Friday only.
- **Early May bank holiday, Monday 3 May**.
- **Spring bank holiday, Monday 31 May**.
- **Christmas**: Christmas Day is a Saturday and Boxing Day a Sunday, so **Monday 27 and Tuesday 28 December** are the substitute days. That is four days off, 25 to 28 December.

Only in some parts:

- **Summer bank holiday**: Monday 30 August in England, Wales and Northern Ireland, Monday 2 August in Scotland.
- **Scotland**: 2 January is a Saturday, so the substitute day is **Monday 4 January**. St Andrew's Day is Tuesday 30 November.
- **Northern Ireland**: St Patrick's Day is Wednesday 17 March and the Battle of the Boyne holiday is Monday 12 July.

### The best use of leave

- **Christmas to New Year**: with 25 to 28 December off, three days of leave on 29, 30 and 31 December give you 25 December to 3 January 2028 (1 January 2028 is a Saturday, and the substitute day in England and Wales is Monday 3 January).
- **Easter**: four days of leave from Tuesday 30 March to Friday 2 April turn the Easter weekend into 10 days off, 26 March to 4 April, in England, Wales and Northern Ireland.

UK employers do not have to give bank holidays off, and they can count them in your 5.6 weeks of paid holiday. Check your contract.
