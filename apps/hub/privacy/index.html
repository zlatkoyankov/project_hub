Privacy policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy explains what information may be processed when you visit Zyankov.eu, Letterwell, or BuildCalc. It describes the current site setup; please check it again whenever a project or third-party service changes.

Who is responsible for your information?

The data controller for Zyankov.eu is Zlatko Yankov, based in Bulgaria. For privacy questions or requests, contact hello@zyankov.eu.

Information processed when you visit

The hosting provider that serves these static pages may process technical request data such as your IP address, browser and device details, requested URL, and request time to deliver and secure the service. The site owner does not receive calculator inputs or word searches through an application backend. Hosting-provider logs are subject to the provider's own retention and privacy practices.

Fonts and images are served from zyankov.eu itself. Pages do not request fonts from Google Fonts or other font services.

Project data and local storage

Letterwell processes the letters you enter in your browser using a dictionary downloaded from this site. The letters are not sent to Zyankov.eu.

Zyankov.eu saves the selected light or dark theme in browser local storage, and the choice applies to every project on the site. Letterwell also saves the selected language, and BuildCalc saves the measurement system. These preferences stay in your browser and are not sent to the site owner. You can remove them by clearing this site's stored data in your browser settings.

Letterwell also keeps up to five recent searches in temporary page memory so you can reuse them while the page remains open. This search history is not saved to local storage and is cleared when you leave or reload the page.

Advertising and cookies

The Google AdSense publisher script is included on the Zyankov.eu homepage, this policy page, and Letterwell. The script makes a request to Google. Google may process technical information such as your IP address, browser or device details, and may use cookies or similar technologies for advertising-related purposes. Whether ads are served, and which technologies are used, can depend on Google's and the publisher-account settings. BuildCalc does not currently load the AdSense script. We do not use a separate analytics service.

Zyankov.eu does not currently provide a cookie or advertising-consent control. This page is not a consent mechanism. Advertising and consent settings must be configured to meet applicable requirements before advertising is enabled for visitors in locations where consent is required.

You can manage Google ad personalization at My Ad Center and learn about other opt-out choices at YourAdChoices.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can control local storage through your browser settings. Depending on where you live, applicable data-protection laws may give you rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection to certain processing.

For a privacy question or request, contact hello@zyankov.eu.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the site or its services change. The date at the top shows when it was most recently reviewed.
