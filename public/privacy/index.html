Your information

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy explains how Zyankov.eu handles information when you visit this website and its projects. The site currently does not display advertising or use analytics.

Who is responsible for your information?

The data controller for Zyankov.eu is Zlatko Yankov, based in Bulgaria. For privacy questions or requests, contact hello@zyankov.eu.

Information processed when you visit

The hosting and security services that deliver the site may process technical information such as your IP address, browser details, requested pages, and request times to operate and protect the service. Their retention periods depend on the hosting configuration.

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those fonts, Google may receive technical request information such as your IP address. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Project data and local storage

Letterwell processes the letters you enter in your browser using a dictionary downloaded from this site. The letters are not sent to Zyankov.eu.

Zyankov.eu and Letterwell save your light or dark theme choice in browser local storage. This preference stays in your browser and is not used to identify you. You can remove it by clearing this site's stored data in your browser settings.

Letterwell also keeps up to five recent searches in temporary page memory so you can reuse them while the page remains open. This search history is not saved to local storage and is cleared when you leave or reload the page.

Advertising and cookies

No ads are currently shown on Zyankov.eu or Letterwell. If Google AdSense or another advertising service is added, advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies and process device or usage information to deliver, measure, and protect ads. Where permitted and based on your choices, this may include personalized advertising.

Before advertising is enabled, this policy and the site's consent controls will be updated to describe the services in use. Where required by Google, a Google-certified consent management platform integrated with the IAB Europe's Transparency and Consent Framework will be configured before ads are served to users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland.

You can manage Google ad personalization at My Ad Center and learn about other opt-out choices at YourAdChoices.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can control local storage through your browser settings. If you are in a region with data-protection rights, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection to certain processing, subject to applicable law.

For a privacy question or request, contact hello@zyankov.eu.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the site or its services change. The date at the top shows when it was most recently reviewed.
